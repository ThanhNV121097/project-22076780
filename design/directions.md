# Trà Sen Hồ Tây — two directions

## The reading

This is a shop on a named street — 58 Quán Thánh, Ba Đình — that blends its own loose-leaf
tea and sells it in 100g and 250g tins, with a few tables where someone can sit over a pot.
Who buys here: people in Ba Đình who buy tea the way they buy rice, and Hanoians buying
lotus tea as a gift, where the giver has to be sure of the blend. How they decide: half
arrive knowing the word — sen, 250g — and want the price and the hours and nothing else;
the other half sit down and ask what is good this season, and buy what they are told.
What it sells on: taste and trust in one person's blending, plus the plain fact of a fixed
address open 8:00–21:00 every day. What it is not: it is not an online shop (the owner said
so), not a café chain with syrups, and not a tourist tea ceremony — so no basket, no
booking, no "experience". The site has one job: show the teas and their prices, show the
room, and put the phone number within a thumb's reach.

Both directions below follow from that. They differ on which half of the customer they
answer first: the one who wants the board, or the one who wants the room.

## Prices on the board

- **Suits you if:** most of your customers already know they want sen or jasmine and only
  need to see the weight, the price and that you are open — you want the website to work
  like the board behind your counter, not like a brochure.
- **Palette (light):** ground `#F4F1E8`, surface `#FFFFFF`, ink `#17180F`, ink-soft
  `#5B5F4E`, accent `#1F5134` (tea-leaf green), accent-ink `#FFFFFF`, line
  `rgba(23, 24, 15, 0.16)`.
- **Faces:** display **Archivo** (grotesk, OFL — a shop sign, not a magazine), body
  **Inter**. Unlock used: a grotesk with real character for the display face, carried
  through every heading and every price.
- **Scale at 1440:** h1 44px / 1.05, tracking -0.02em; h2 28px; prices set as display
  figures at 34px, tabular, right-aligned so the digits line up down the column.
- **First screen:** no hero photograph — the left third is the name, the street and the
  hours with a call button; the right two thirds are the three blends as a board, each tin
  cut out on its own tile, both weights priced on one hairline-ruled line, and a photograph
  of the leaf running off the right edge above them.
- **Device a reviewer can point at:** oversized tabular prices on a hairline rule system —
  one rule weight, one column of digits, nothing else drawn.

## A pot at the table

- **Suits you if:** people come in because of the room — they sit down, ask what is good
  today and leave with a tin — and you would rather the website make someone want to walk
  up Quán Thánh than compare prices before they do.
- **Palette (dark):** ground `#14110E`, surface `#1F1A15`, ink `#F3EEE4`, ink-soft
  `#A99C8B`, accent `#D98BA0` (lotus), accent-ink `#1A1310`, line
  `rgba(243, 238, 228, 0.14)`.
- **Faces:** display **Instrument Serif** (OFL, regular 400 — the one serif, for a room
  that is older than the shop), body **Inter**. Unlock used: a display face that is not a
  sans, carried through every heading on every page.
- **Scale at 1440:** h1 64px / 1.05, tracking -0.015em; h2 36px; prices 20px tabular in
  the accent, small-caps weights beside them.
- **First screen:** a full-bleed photograph of the table and the pour, the words set low
  on the left over a scrim, the hours and the phone on the same edge, and a dark rail down
  the right holding the three blends as cut-out tins with their prices.
- **Device a reviewer can point at:** the photograph bleeds the whole screen with a
  small-caps label set vertically up the left edge over it.

CHOSEN: Prices on the board — half of this shop's customers already know the word they
want (sen, 250g) and only need the weight, the price and the fact that it is open, so the
site is built to work like the board behind the counter rather than a brochure.

## Notes

- Prices shown on both pages are **sample prices** — the owner has not supplied a price
  list. The three blends (lotus, jasmine, oolong) and the two tin sizes are the owner's.
- Phone, Zalo, address and hours are the owner's own, used exactly as written.
- Photographs are stock stand-ins, cut out for the tins so every priced item has a picture.
- Looked at for structure and timing only, nothing taken from their identities:
  land-book.com and awwwards.com listings, Cal.com, Linear, Are.na.
